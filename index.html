This Privacy Policy explains how FROGVEXA GAMES handles information in the Block Pulse mobile game.

1. Information and Game Progress

Block Pulse uses local device storage to support game progress, settings, coins, unlocked content, and other gameplay preferences. Local game data may be lost if the application is uninstalled or its storage is cleared.

2. Advertising

Block Pulse integrates Google AdMob to display rewarded advertisements. Google's advertising services may collect or process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, ad interaction information, and diagnostic information, depending on device settings and applicable consent choices.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Block Pulse is designed to offer optional purchases through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes payments according to its own policies. The game may process purchase status and entitlement information to unlock purchased content or restore eligible purchases. The developer does not receive complete payment card details through Google Play Billing.

4. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the game and its service providers may be used to provide gameplay features, deliver advertisements, support purchases, prevent fraud, maintain security, and diagnose technical problems.

5. Third-Party Services

Block Pulse integrates services provided by Google, including Google AdMob and Google Play. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress stored locally can generally be removed by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the game. Data processed by Google services is subject to the applicable provider's retention and deletion policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Block Pulse is not specifically designed for children. The game's intended audience and any applicable age restrictions will be reflected in its Google Play listing and advertising settings.

8. Security

We aim to use appropriate safeguards for the information handled by the game. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game, its services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page.
